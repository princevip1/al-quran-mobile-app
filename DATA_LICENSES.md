## Important Notes

1. **Accuracy:** We strive to ensure the accuracy of all Quranic text and translations. If you find any errors, please report them to us.

2. **Non-Commercial Use:** This app is provided free of charge and is not intended for commercial use. All data sources have been verified to be freely available for non-commercial Islamic educational purposes.

3. **Respect for Sources:** We deeply respect and acknowledge the work of all scholars, translators, and organizations who have made their work available for the benefit of the Muslim community.

4. **Updates:** Data sources may be updated periodically to ensure accuracy and include improvements.

5. **Offline Access:** All data is stored locally on your device to enable offline access, in accordance with the terms of use of each source.
